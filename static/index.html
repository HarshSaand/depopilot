What this result proves—and its limits

The Gaussian process uses only the displayed observation set. Expected target-error improvement selects one of 601 experimental recipes. A reveal retrieves a historical measurement; it does not run a chamber. Predictive intervals are model estimates and are not calibrated guarantees. Timing and campaign bounds are checked; tool safety, film quality and uniformity are unverified.

Source: Bipolar HiPIMS dataset (CC BY 4.0). Al calibration: raw rate × 1000 × 1.1684. Å/s = angstroms per second.
